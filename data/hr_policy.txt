# HR COMPANY POLICY
**Company:** Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

## 1. Working Hours
- Office hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday.
- Employees must complete 8 working hours daily.
- Employees must arrive on time.
- Employees can request flexible working hours with manager approval.
- Lunch break is 1 hour.

## 2. Leave Policy
- Employees receive 18 paid leaves annually.
- Employees receive 10 sick leaves annually.
- Employees must request planned leave at least 3 days in advance.
- Emergency leave must be communicated to the reporting manager.
- Unused annual leaves can be carried forward up to 5 days.
- Leave approval depends on manager approval.

## 3. Work From Home
- Employees can work from home 2 days per week.
- Prior manager approval is required.
- Employees must remain available during working hours.
- Company laptops must be used for official work.
- Employees must maintain productivity while working remotely.

## 4. Attendance Policy
- Employees must mark attendance using the company attendance system.
- Late arrivals must be reported to the manager.
- Repeated unapproved absences may result in disciplinary action.
- Employees must inform their manager if they cannot attend work.

## 5. Salary Policy
- Salaries are paid on the last working day of every month.
- Salary details are mentioned in the employment offer letter.
- Employees receive monthly payslips.
- Salary-related issues should be reported to the HR department.
- Applicable statutory deductions are made from salaries.

## 6. Employee Code of Conduct
- Employees must behave professionally.
- Harassment, discrimination, and bullying are prohibited.
- Employees must respect colleagues and management.
- Company resources must not be misused.
- Confidential company information must not be shared externally.
- Employees must disclose conflicts of interest.

## 7. Probation Policy
- New employees have a probation period of 6 months.
- Performance is evaluated during probation.
- Probation may be extended based on performance.
- Successful employees receive written confirmation.
- Employment conditions are governed by the employment agreement.

## 8. Resignation Policy
- Employees must submit resignation through official email.
- Notice period is determined by the employment agreement.
- Employees must complete pending tasks before leaving.
- Company equipment must be returned.
- HR conducts an exit process before the employee leaves.
- Final settlement is processed after separation according to applicable terms and law.

## 9. Performance Evaluation
- Employee performance is reviewed twice annually.
- Evaluations consider productivity, teamwork, communication, and work quality.
- Managers provide feedback during performance reviews.
- Promotions depend on performance and business requirements.
- Employees may be assigned additional training to improve performance.

## 10. Data Security Policy
- Employees must protect confidential company information.
- Passwords must never be shared.
- Unauthorized software installation is prohibited.
- Company data must not be stored on unauthorized personal devices.
- Security incidents must be reported immediately to IT.
- Employees must use approved AI tools when handling company information.

## 11. Training Policy
- New employees receive onboarding training.
- Employees may attend technical and professional development programs.
- Training requests require manager approval.
- Mandatory security and compliance training must be completed.

## 12. Disciplinary Policy
- Policy violations may result in warnings or disciplinary action.
- Serious misconduct may result in termination.
- Employees are given an opportunity to respond to allegations.
- HR reviews disciplinary matters before decisions are finalized.
